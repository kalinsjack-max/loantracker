Help & FAQ

How do I add a loan?
Use the form on the dashboard. Choose Lent or Borrowed, enter the person, amount and dates. Select a registered user to send them a confirmation request; otherwise the entry stays personal.
What does Pending mean?
The other person has not accepted yet. Pending entries are not counted in your totals.
How do partial payments work?
Press the repay / receive button and enter an amount. For shared entries the other side must confirm it.
How do reminders work?
Overdue and soon-due entries are highlighted on the dashboard and shown when you open the app. Enable browser reminders in Tools.
Can I export my data?
Yes: CSV, a printable PDF statement, or a full JSON download from Tools or your profile.
How do I delete my account?
Profile → Account → Delete account. Your profile and private details are erased.
